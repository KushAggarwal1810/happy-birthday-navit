A FEW WORDS. ALL MY HEART.12 / 09

Mere pyaare Navit,

Happy Birthday, mere chhote bhai. 🤍

Iss page par jo pehli photo hai na… tu meri god mein hai. Itna sa. Ek blanket mein lipta hua, apni chhoti si duniya mein aaraam se so raha hai. Shayad tujhe woh pal yaad nahi hoga. Par mere liye woh koi ordinary photo nahi hai.

Woh ek reminder hai ki kuch log humari zindagi mein bahut chhote se aate hain… aur dil mein bahut badi jagah bana lete hain.

Tu mere liye wahi hai.

Technically, main tera Chacha hoon. Family tree ko dekhoge toh wahi likha milega. Par apna equation thoda alag hai, na? Tu mera chhota bhai hai. Mera teammate. Meri bakwaas ka future partner. Aur meri zindagi ki un khushiyon mein se ek, jinka main shayad roz zikr nahi karta… par dil se bahut shukraguzaar hoon.

Teri photos dekh raha tha. Kabhi woh tiny little shoes. Kabhi panda wali cap. Kabhi Kanha bana hua. Kabhi Spider-Man. Aur kabhi bas tu — apni poori masti mein, bina duniya ki kisi tension ke.

Bas uss wale Navit ko kabhi khona mat.
Jo chhoti-chhoti cheezon mein badi khushi dhoond leta hai.

Tu bada hoga. Teri duniya badi hogi. Naye dost, naye sapne, naye sawaal. Kuch din aise honge jab lagega sab possible hai. Aur kuch din aise bhi, jab kuch samajh nahi aayega.

Dono tarah ke dinon ke liye yeh baat yaad rakhna: tujhe mere paas aane ke liye perfect hone ki zaroorat nahi hai. Na top karna zaroori hai. Na hamesha strong banna. Na har problem ka answer pata hona.

Khushi ho toh bata dena. Kuch galat ho gaya ho toh bhi. Advice chahiye toh dunga. Sirf sunne wala chahiye toh chup-chaap sununga. Aur agar bas thodi der bakwaas karke mann halka karna ho… uske liye toh main waise bhi ready hoon.

Meri wish tere liye sirf success nahi hai. Main chahta hoon tu dil ka achha insaan bane. Apne se chhote ko izzat de. Kisi akele insaan ko apne saath bitha le. Galti ho toh sorry bol sake. Aur apni khushi ke liye jagah banana kabhi na bhoole.

Dream big, mere bhai. Par apne aap se pyaar sirf tab mat karna jab koi dream poora ho. Tu aaj bhi kaafi hai. Abhi, jaisa hai, waise hi.

Ek din shayad tu mujhse lamba ho jaayega. Shayad mere jokes pe aankhein ghumayega. Shayad kahega, “Bhai, aap bhi na…” Aur main hasunga. Kyunki mere liye, kahin na kahin, tu hamesha woh chhota sa bachcha bhi rahega jo meri god mein aaraam se so raha tha.

Yeh website ussi pyaar ka ek chhota sa backup hai. Taaki saalon baad bhi, jab tu ise khole, tujhe yaad aaye: you have always been loved. Long before you had to become anything.

Ab emotional speech khatam. Cake khaa. Masti kar. Photos le. Aur woh wali badi si smile de — jis wajah se yeh saari mehnat worth it hai.

Happy Birthday, Navit.
Bada zaroor hona. Par apne andar ki roshni ko kabhi chhota mat hone dena.
